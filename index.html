Frequently asked questions

Are these tools free?

Yes. The browser-based tools on this site are free to use.

Are my files uploaded?

Most tools process files locally in your browser. If a server conversion option is configured, the site will tell you before sending a file.

Will PDF to Word keep the original layout?

Browser-based PDF text extraction focuses on editable text and may not preserve complex formatting, columns, tables or images. Use a dedicated desktop converter for exact layout reproduction.
